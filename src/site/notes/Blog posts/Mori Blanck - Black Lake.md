<p style="text-align: center;"><i>Mori Blanck - Black Lake</i></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><i>2026</i></p>

While I was digging through [tottomori](https://www.tottomori.com/)'s discography, I stumbledupon this gem of a grungy alt-rock album. Under his alter ego _Mori Blanck_ he deviated from his usual ambient soundtrack tunes and made a more band-like record. Full of roaring electric guitars and distorted, yet melodic riffs, it brings an odd, melancholic harmony into chaos in the best way possible. It reminds me a lot of the shoegaze movement.

My favorite tracks off the album are the energetic opener Havregryn (oatmeal in English) and the more calm, melodic Squidheart. Truly a hidden gem of modern indie rock that is reminiscing of older times while bringing a fresh spin on the genre. Comfortable and yet grainy, it's a "I could listen to it almost every day" 10/10 album for me that keeps my hope for the future of this kind of music alive.
