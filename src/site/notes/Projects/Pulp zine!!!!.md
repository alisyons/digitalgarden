This is the info page for a little community project called _Pulp Zine_! It's a print-it-yourself 16-page zine made by and for fans of the band Pulp, moderated by [Dzena](https://instagram.com/cherryycolouredfffunk), [Leo](https://instagram.com/leoju_s) and [me](https://instagram.com/alisyons).
## How do I participate?

Just hit me up on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/alisyons) and I will send you an invite to our Discord server! 

## What kind of contributions are you looking for?

Artworks, collages, poems... _anything goes!_ Just keep in mind that it will be black/white and pretty small _(A7 format when bound)_.

## When's the deadline?

We are aiming for distribution at the gigs in Berlin and Cologne, so for us to be able to compile everything in time, please submit your contribution until _May 31_.

### _✦ When the project is done, everyone can download and print out the zine! If we could get some zine distributors for the other shows, that would be amazing. Spread the Pulp love! ✦_
